Blade Spin - Privacy Policy

App package com.nnastudio.bladespin · Last updated 22 November 2026

Short version

Blade Spin collects no personal data: no name, no email, no account, no advertising ID. To improve the game it sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics, such as which stage a run reached. Your progress stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress - best score, stage reached, apples, unlocked knives, power-ups, and the sound and vibration switches - is saved in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Anonymous usage statistics

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how the game is played, so levels and rewards can be balanced. It records game events (a run started or ended and the stage reached, a boss beaten, a challenge cleared, a knife case opened, a daily reward claimed) together with basic technical information (device model, Android version, app version, country, and an app-instance ID created by Firebase). The game does not collect the advertising ID, and none of this identifies you personally. Google processes the data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Firebase keeps analytics data for at most 14 months.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID and no ad tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared beyond Google acting as our analytics provider.

Ratings

After you have played for a while, the game may show Google Play's own rating window at a good moment, such as a new best score. It appears at most a few times; whether you rate, and what you write, is handled entirely by Google Play.

Permissions

VIBRATE, for the short buzz when a knife lands or a run ends (you can turn it off in Settings), and internet access, used only to send the anonymous statistics above. The game is fully playable offline.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13; the statistics above are anonymous and not used for advertising.

Purchases

The current version contains no in-app purchases. If that ever changes, payment is handled entirely by Google Play and this policy will be updated before the feature ships.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text ships with the game update and is published with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy, including deleting analytics data: email newtrust.contact@gmail.com. Tapping FEEDBACK in Settings opens your mail app with the address filled in.
